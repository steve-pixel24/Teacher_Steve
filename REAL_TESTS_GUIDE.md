## 💡 Test-Taking Tips

### General Strategy:
1. **Read the question carefully** - Understand what's being asked
2. **Eliminate obvious wrong answers** - Narrow down choices
3. **Look for keywords** - They often indicate the correct answer
4. **Use context clues** - The question itself may provide hints
5. **Trust your first instinct** - Don't overthink
6. **Learn from explanations** - Understand why answers are correct

### For Grammar Tests:
- Identify the tense or structure being tested
- Look for time markers (yesterday, tomorrow, since, etc.)
- Check subject-verb agreement
- Consider the context (formal/informal, spoken/written)

### For Vocabulary Tests:
- Think about word roots and prefixes
- Consider the context of the sentence
- Eliminate answers that don't fit grammatically
- Use process of elimination

### For Business English Tests:
- Think about professional contexts
- Consider formality levels
- Remember common business phrases
- Focus on practical usage
