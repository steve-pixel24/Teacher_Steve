## 🎯 New Features: Comprehension & Vocabulary Exercises

### 📝 Comprehension Questions
Each story now includes **4-6 comprehension questions** in three formats:

1. **Multiple Choice** (3-4 options)
   - Tests reading comprehension
   - Immediate feedback with explanations
   - Example: "Why was the narrator feeling overwhelmed?"

2. **True/False**
   - Quick fact-checking
   - Tests attention to detail
   - Example: "True or False: The narrator gave up after the first fall."

3. **Open-Ended**
   - Encourages critical thinking
   - Provides suggested answers for teachers
   - Example: "What lesson did the narrator learn that day?"

**XP Rewards:** Up to 30 XP per story based on correct answers

---

### 📚 Vocabulary Exercises
Each story includes **4-5 vocabulary exercises** in three formats:

1. **Fill-in-the-Blank**
   - Students type the correct word
   - Context-based learning
   - Example: "I felt completely ______ by the noise and crowds."

2. **Matching**
   - Match word with synonym/definition
   - Multiple choice format
   - Example: Match "devastated" with "extremely sad"

3. **Context**
   - Understand word usage in different situations
   - Tests deeper comprehension
   - Example: "In which situation would you use 'breakthrough'?"

**XP Rewards:** Up to 20 XP per story based on correct answers

## 🎯 Difficulty Levels Explained

### A2 (Elementary)
- **Sentence Structure:** Simple sentences, basic grammar
- **Vocabulary:** Common, everyday words
- **Topics:** Familiar situations (school, family, hobbies)
- **Length:** 300-500 words
- **Examples:** "Learning to Ride a Bike," "My First Day at a New School"

### B1 (Intermediate)
- **Sentence Structure:** Mix of simple and complex sentences
- **Vocabulary:** More varied, some idiomatic expressions
- **Topics:** Travel, culture, relationships, work
- **Length:** 500-800 words
- **Examples:** "My First Train Journey in Japan," "Dinner with a Brazilian Family," "Living with Strangers in London"

### B2 (Upper Intermediate)
- **Sentence Structure:** Complex sentences, varied grammar
- **Vocabulary:** Advanced, topic-specific terminology
- **Topics:** Professional life, technology, personal development
- **Length:** 800-1,200 words
- **Examples:** "The Reality of Working from Home," "From Idea to Reality," "Thirty Days Without Social Media"

### C1 (Advanced)
- **Sentence Structure:** Sophisticated, nuanced grammar
- **Vocabulary:** Academic, abstract, specialized
- **Topics:** Social issues, philosophy, complex themes
- **Length:** 1,200-1,500+ words
- **Examples:** "The Weight of Tomorrow: A Climate Activist's Journey"
